# Before the Bell 🔔

**Before the Bell** is a story-driven visual novel made with **Ren’Py**.

What starts as an ordinary school day slowly turns into something much stranger. After a mysterious incident, Sasha finds herself trapped in a repeating version of her school, where familiar faces, forgotten memories, and unexplained events begin to connect.

As the story unfolds, Sasha must uncover what happened, understand the truth behind the loop, and find a way to break it.

 **Made with:** Ren’Py
 **Genre:** Mystery • Drama • Supernatural
 **Type:** Story-driven Visual Novel
